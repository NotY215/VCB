# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this project to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good faith during technical disagreements.
- Focus criticism on code, designs, and ideas rather than people.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Hate speech or discriminatory conduct.
- Personal attacks or targeted insults.
- Sexual harassment.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the project maintainers through GitHub.

Reports will be reviewed as reasonably and confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when conduct violates this policy.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces where participants are representing or contributing to the project.

## Good-Faith Participation

Technical disagreement is welcome. The goal is to resolve disagreements through evidence, testing, documentation, and respectful discussion.
